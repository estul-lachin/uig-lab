TLP:CLEAR  -  Incident summary IR-2026-117  -  Hollow Pine Freight (fictional)

Summary
-------
Between 2026-09-02 and 2026-09-19 an affiliate of the NightTide ransomware-as-a-service operation,
tracked as "tern", compromised Hollow Pine Freight, stole about 180 GB of data and encrypted the
file servers. NightTide's developers run the payment portal and the leak site; the affiliate did the
intrusion. Access was not gained by tern itself: on 2026-09-02 a broker on a criminal forum offered
"VPN access, logistics, 2k staff" and the listing matches this victim.

Observed infrastructure (defanged)
----------------------------------
- Scanning of the VPN portal came from 198.51.100[.]23.
- The VPN login with stolen credentials came from 203.0.113[.]14.
- The loader was fetched from hxxps://update-cdn[.]top/a.ps1 ; update-cdn[.]top resolved to 192.0.2[.]77
  and was also the beacon destination (HTTPS, every 60 s).
- The leak site is ntide4leaksx7q[.]onion ; negotiation happens at chat.ntide-support[.]top .
- Data was uploaded with a renamed rclone to a public cloud storage service.

Extortion
---------
After encryption the victim's name appeared on the leak site with a countdown. Three days later the
customer portal was hit by a DDoS attack and several of Hollow Pine's customers received e-mails
telling them their shipment data would be published.
