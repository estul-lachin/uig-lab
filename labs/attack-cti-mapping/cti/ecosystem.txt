THE ATT&CK ECOSYSTEM - name the project or term each describes. Record them
as ATOMIC, EMULATION, DEFENSE, PURPLE, STIX in /root/answers.txt.

 ATOMIC    : an open-source library of small, single-technique tests, each
             mapped to one ATT&CK technique (by Red Canary).
 EMULATION : MITRE's platform that chains techniques into full, automated
             adversary-emulation operations.
 DEFENSE   : MITRE's knowledge graph of DEFENSIVE countermeasures - the
             counterpart to ATT&CK's offensive view.
 PURPLE    : what it's called when red (emulation) and blue (detection) work
             together on ATT&CK common ground.
 STIX      : the machine-readable format ATT&CK is published in so SIEMs/TIPs
             can ingest it (two-part name; either part is accepted).
